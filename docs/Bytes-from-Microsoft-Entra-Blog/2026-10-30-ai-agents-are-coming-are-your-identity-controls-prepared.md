# AI agents are coming—are your identity controls prepared?

*Published 30 October 2026*

## Summary

Microsoft has published new guidance on securing enterprise identities to prepare for the risks and demands introduced by AI. The recommendations focus on adopting phishing-resistant MFA, eliminating standing administrative privileges using Entra PIM, and applying comprehensive policy controls across all user and agent identities.

## Key points

- AI is accelerating identity threats like automated phishing and credential theft.
- Organizations must transition all users and administrators to phishing-resistant MFA such as passkeys or FIDO2 keys.
- Standing privileges should be eliminated by implementing just-in-time, time-bound access through Microsoft Entra PIM.
- Conditional Access policies and Entra ID Protection must be applied broadly to govern both human and emerging AI agent identities.


!!! warning "Action required"
    IT and identity administrators should review the new Microsoft guidance and implement phishing-resistant MFA and Privileged Identity Management best practices.
