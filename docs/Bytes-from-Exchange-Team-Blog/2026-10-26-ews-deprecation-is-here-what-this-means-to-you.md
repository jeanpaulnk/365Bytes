## From the article

> The deprecation of EWS in Exchange Online starts today. This is a multi-step process, years in the making, and this post has lots of important information. We will keep this post updated if we have new information to add. The first change, as previously announced , starting early October 2026, means setting EWSEnabled to True will no longer be sufficient on its own to keep EWS working in the tenant: an AppID allow list (EWSAllowedAppIDs) will be required to specify apps that you want to permit access to EWS.
