# Copilot at Microsoft Ignite: Expanding what’s possible with intelligence built for work

*Published 17 November 2026*

## Summary

At Microsoft Ignite 2026, Microsoft announced the general availability of Copilot Home, Office in Copilot, and the Copilot Managed Runtime, alongside previews for features like Today and autonomous agents like Autopilot. The updates bring multi-model auto-routing, enhanced Work IQ reasoning over Microsoft 365 data, and expanded enterprise controls through Agent 365.

## Key points

- Copilot Home (combining Chat and Cowork) and Office in Copilot are now generally available.
- Autopilot, a personal autonomous agent, is beginning to roll out to Frontier customers.
- Auto routing dynamically selects the best industry model for tasks to balance quality, speed, and cost.
- Agent 365 expands management controls, Entra Agent ID governance, and introduces FinOps for AI analytics.
- New plugins and prebuilt skills for Dynamics 365, Salesforce, finance, and legal teams enhance multi-system workflows.
