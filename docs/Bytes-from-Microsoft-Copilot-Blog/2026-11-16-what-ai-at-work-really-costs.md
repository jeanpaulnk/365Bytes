# What AI at work really costs

*Published 16 November 2026*

## Summary

Microsoft shared commissioned research from GigaOm evaluating the total cost of ownership for enterprise AI products like Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT Enterprise, and Claude Enterprise. The study concluded that Microsoft Copilot offered the lowest monthly cost for both everyday and advanced users while maintaining comparable output quality. The cost advantages were attributed to subscription structures for everyday tasks, Microsoft 365 contextual awareness via Work IQ, and automated model routing.

## Key points

- Commissioned GigaOm research compared the total cost of ownership of Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT Enterprise, and Claude Enterprise.
- The study found that Copilot had the lowest monthly cost for both everyday and advanced users compared to competitors.
- Everyday AI tasks are included in the base Copilot subscription, avoiding per-task usage charges.
- Work IQ and automatic model routing help reduce context retrieval overhead and optimize compute costs.
