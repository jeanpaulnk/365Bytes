# Tracking AI adoption in North Korean threat actor tradecraft

*Published 29 October 2026*

## Summary

Microsoft Threat Intelligence tracks how North Korean threat groups are incorporating generative and agentic AI into their operations to scale social engineering, reconnaissance, and malware development. While threat actors use AI as a force multiplier to accelerate workflows, these operations remain human-directed rather than fully autonomous. The report categorizes various groups by their level of AI adoption and provides context on their evolving tactics.

## Key points

- North Korean threat actors are leveraging AI to accelerate reconnaissance, target research, and social engineering.
- Groups like Jade Sleet and Coral Sleet have operationalized AI across their workflows, deploying rapid front companies and trojanized repositories.
- AI tools are increasingly used for generating phishing lures, synthetic media, and assisting in malware and tooling development.
- Microsoft assesses that AI usage serves to scale existing North Korean tradecraft rather than change underlying state objectives.
