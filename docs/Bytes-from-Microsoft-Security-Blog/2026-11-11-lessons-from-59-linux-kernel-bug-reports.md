# Lessons from 59 Linux Kernel Bug Reports

*Published 11 November 2026*

## Summary

Microsoft's FORGE Lab details how they used AI tools to scan subsets of the Linux kernel, resulting in 59 public upstream reports, 33 CVEs, and insights into scaling vulnerability discovery. The research highlights the challenges of filtering raw AI findings, validating them, and working with maintainers to merge fixes.

## Key points

- AI-powered scans of Linux kernel subsystems produced thousands of raw findings, leading to 59 public reports and 33 CVEs.
- Fixes for 38 of the reported issues have been merged into upstream mainline after human review and iteration.
- The research underscores that while AI efficiently discovers potential bugs, human validation and patching remain significant bottlenecks.
