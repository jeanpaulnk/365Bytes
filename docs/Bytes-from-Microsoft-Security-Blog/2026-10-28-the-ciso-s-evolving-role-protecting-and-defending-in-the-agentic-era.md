## Summary

This article discusses the changing responsibilities of Chief Information Security Officers (CISOs) in an enterprise environment increasingly driven by AI agents. It highlights the rapid adoption of AI builders, the necessity of strong identity and data governance for agents, and how security teams must modernize their defenses to match AI-powered threats.

## Key points

- Active AI agents in the Microsoft 365 ecosystem have grown exponentially over the past year.
- CISOs must balance empowering rapid business innovation with establishing robust agent governance and observability.
- Developers building AI agents should assign them governed identities and carefully restrict data access early in the process.
- Defenders need integrated security tools, threat intelligence, and defensive AI agents to keep pace with fast-moving cyberattacks.
