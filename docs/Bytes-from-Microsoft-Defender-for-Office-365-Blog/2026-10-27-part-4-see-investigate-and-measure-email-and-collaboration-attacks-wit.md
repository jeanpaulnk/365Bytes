# Part 4: See, investigate, and measure email and collaboration attacks with Defender reporting

!!! info "From the Microsoft Defender for Office 365 Blog"
    Based on [this article](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/t5/microsoft-defender-for-office/part-4-see-investigate-and-measure-email-and-collaboration/ba-p/4562274) by IustinIrimia, published 27 October 2026.

## Summary

Microsoft has released version 4 of its Detections and Insights reporting templates for Microsoft Sentinel and Power BI. The update introduces new Teams and collaboration threat views, 125 new or updated advanced hunting queries, and a dedicated Attack Simulation Training Insights report to help administrators track user resilience.

## Key points

- Released version 4 of Detections and Insights templates for Microsoft Sentinel and Power BI.
- Added a new Teams &amp; Collaboration view and Email Impersonation tab to track multi-channel attacks.
- Introduced 125 new or improved advanced hunting queries for email and Microsoft Teams.
- Added a new Attack Simulation Training Insights report to measure user awareness and progress.
- Templates and queries are available now via GitHub, Sentinel Content Hub, and the Defender portal.


!!! warning "Action required"
    Microsoft 365 and security administrators should update their Microsoft Defender XDR solution in Sentinel, download the new Power BI templates, and deploy the updated workbooks and hunting queries from GitHub if they wish to use the new reporting features.
