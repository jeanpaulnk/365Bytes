# Office LTSC 2021 support ends today

*Published 13 October 2026*

## Summary

Support for Office LTSC 2021 suites and standalone apps officially ended today, October 13, 2026. Microsoft advises administrators to migrate affected devices to either cloud-based Microsoft 365 plans or upgrade to the newer Office LTSC 2024 for disconnected environments.

## Key points

- Office LTSC 2021 and all associated standalone applications are now out of support.
- No further security updates, bug fixes, or technical support will be provided for these versions.
- Organizations are encouraged to migrate to Microsoft 365 apps or upgrade to Office LTSC 2024.
- FastTrack App Assure is available for eligible organizations needing help with line-of-business app compatibility.


!!! warning "Action required"
    IT administrators running Office LTSC 2021 must upgrade or migrate those devices to a supported product version immediately.
