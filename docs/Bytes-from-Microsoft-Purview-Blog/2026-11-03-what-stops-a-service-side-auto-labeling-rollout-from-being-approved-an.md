# What stops a service-side auto-labeling rollout from being approved — and how to unblock it

*Published 3 November 2026*

## Summary

This article outlines the common roadblocks in getting service-side auto-labeling policies approved and offers strategies to unblock them. It breaks down eight key deployment scenarios ranging from access impacts to large tenant scoping, and highlights crucial prerequisites, limits, and rollback planning.

## Key points

- Address access and encryption concerns by separating classification-only labels from restricted encryption policies.
- Prepare comprehensive approval packs including owner-reviewed samples, simulation exports, and tested rollback runbooks.
- Understand Purview limits, such as daily labeling maximums, adaptive scope thresholds, and simulation match ceilings.
- Manage policy conflicts and priorities carefully, as auto-labeling policies interact with existing manual and automated labels.
